# Sky Designers SEO Content Strategy

Target market: Sri Lanka

Phase: Content SEO and topic clusters

Prepared: 31 August 2026

Related ownership map: [`docs/seo-keyword-map.md`](./seo-keyword-map.md)

## Strategy principles

- Service pages retain the primary commercial intent defined in the keyword map. Blog posts answer narrower questions, comparisons, cost considerations, implementation problems, and evidence-led lessons.
- The homepage remains the primary page for **digital marketing agency Sri Lanka**. No article should be written as a substitute agency landing page.
- Articles should lead readers to the most relevant service page, not only to the homepage or contact anchor.
- Work links are recommended only where a published work item genuinely supports the article. A portfolio item is not automatically a case study and must not be presented as proof of results unless verified results exist.
- Sri Lankan context should be useful: LKR budgeting, local buying journeys, mobile use, multilingual communication, local business constraints, and platform availability where relevant. Do not add unsupported market statistics.
- Evidence-led articles must use verified campaign records, approved screenshots, and client permissions. Results must name the measurement period, attribution basis, spend, and limitations.

## Search-intent boundaries

| Page type | Owns | Blog support must target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Homepage `/` | General agency selection intent | Evaluation criteria, brief preparation, decision frameworks, and practical comparisons |
| Service pages | Specialist commercial service intent | Costs, process, mistakes, how-to guidance, comparisons, and supporting case analysis |
| Packages `/packages` | Meta Ads package comparison and selection | Budget factors and what a budget must cover; avoid publishing a competing package catalogue |
| Work `/work` | Portfolio and project discovery | Lessons and process supported by a named project; always link back to the canonical work item |
| Blog `/blog` | Educational discovery hub | Specific informational, commercial-investigation, local, and evidence-led queries |

- [ ] The primary keyword/topic does not duplicate the homepage or service-page primary intent.
- [ ] The title, slug, H1, and angle are distinct from all existing posts.
- [ ] The correct cluster and pillar page are documented.

### Accuracy and usefulness

- [ ] The introduction gives a direct, useful answer.
- [ ] Advice is based on a real decision process, not generic filler.
- [ ] Sri Lankan context changes or improves the advice rather than decorating it.
- [ ] Every statistic, platform claim, price, and market claim has a dated source or internal evidence.
- [ ] Hypothetical examples are labelled; real results include measurement limits.
- [ ] Platform guidance has been checked against current official documentation before publication.

### Evidence and trust

- [ ] Author name and actual role are present.
- [ ] Real experience, screenshots, and metrics have approval.
- [ ] Client-sensitive information is removed.
- [ ] Published and updated dates are accurate.
- [ ] Case studies state dates, spend, attribution, and what the data cannot prove.

### Structure and on-page SEO

- [ ] The page has one H1 and logical H2/H3 hierarchy.
- [ ] The SEO title and description are unique and useful.
- [ ] The slug is short, stable, lowercase, and descriptive.
- [ ] Images are compressed, relevant, and have meaningful alt text.
- [ ] FAQs add real decision value and are not included solely for schema.
- [ ] The conclusion uses a relevant CTA rather than a generic contact prompt.

### Internal linking

- [ ] At least one contextual link points to the correct service pillar.
- [ ] A second cluster article is linked only when genuinely useful.
- [ ] A work link uses the canonical `/work/{slug}` URL and accurately describes what it proves.
- [ ] The pillar page is queued to link back after at least two supporting posts are published.
- [ ] Anchor text is descriptive and varied naturally.
