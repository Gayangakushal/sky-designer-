## Purpose and guardrails

This strategy is designed to improve the authority, trust and discoverability of Sky Designers through accurate business citations, genuine relationships, useful resources and editorially justified links. A backlink is valuable only when it helps a real audience understand Sky Designers, a service, or a documented project.

No opportunity in this document is a guaranteed link. Client, partner, event, publication, directory and association links require approval by the site owner. Membership should be pursued for genuine business value, not solely for a link.

The strategy excludes bulk link buying, private blog networks, automated directory submission, comment or forum spam, scaled reciprocal linking, hidden credits, fake reviews, fake locations or profiles, and irrelevant international directories.

### Outreach sequence for a project opportunity

1. Verify the project page is current, accurate and approved for public use.
2. Identify the client's appropriate contact and a page where the acknowledgement would help visitors.
3. Propose one optional placement and natural wording; do not prescribe anchor text.
4. Offer a reciprocal editorial benefit only when genuine, such as sharing the client's approved case-study quote—not a link exchange.
5. Record permission and the final URL. Thank the contact whether or not they add a link.
6. Recheck the link and surrounding claim during the monthly review.

Website footer credits should be subtle, visible, accessible and client-approved. Never hide a link, inject it without consent, or make it sitewide when a single project acknowledgement would be more appropriate.

## Anchor-text safety

Let the linking site choose language that reads naturally. Suggested wording is optional and should describe the relationship or cited resource.

Preferred patterns:

- **Brand:** `Sky Designers`
- **Brand plus location where useful:** `Sky Designers Sri Lanka`
- **Naked URL:** `https://skydesigners.lk`
- **Descriptive resource:** `website project planning checklist for Sri Lankan SMEs`
- **Project-specific:** `Lulu Bridal Studio & Academy website project`
- **Service/context-specific:** `Sky Designers' video production work` or `the campaign analysis`
- **Natural sentence anchors:** `the planning checklist published by Sky Designers`

Avoid repeatedly requesting exact-match commercial anchors such as `digital marketing agency sri lanka`. Do not prescribe the same anchor to multiple sites, stuff city/service variants into company names, or ask editors to alter natural wording solely for keywords.

A healthy profile grows from different source types and editorial contexts. Brand and naked-URL links should be common for profiles and mentions; descriptive anchors should point to genuinely relevant guides, projects and services. Review patterns qualitatively rather than enforcing artificial percentages. A sudden cluster of identical anchors, unrelated sites or sitewide links is a reason to investigate.

### Outreach controls

- Maintain a small prospect list based on relevance, not scraped volume.
- Review the target page before every message.
- Do not imply an existing relationship, sponsorship or endorsement.
- Do not offer payment for a followed editorial link.
- Do not require reciprocal links.
- Stop after one polite follow-up unless the recipient invites continued discussion.
- Record consent for client names, quotes, screenshots and metrics.

## Monthly backlink monitoring checklist

Create a private monthly log with the review date, reviewer, source, target URL, status and required action. Do not store passwords in the SEO document.

### Google Search Console

- [ ] Export or review the Links report for top linking sites, top linked pages and common anchor text.
- [ ] Compare against the prior month's saved snapshot; Search Console is not a complete real-time backlink index.
- [ ] Inspect unfamiliar domains and links in context before labelling them useful or harmful.
- [ ] Confirm priority resources and project pages are receiving relevant links.

### Bing Webmaster Tools

- [ ] Review the Backlinks report when sufficient data becomes available.
- [ ] Compare newly observed referring pages with Search Console and the outreach log.
- [ ] Treat differences between tools as normal; neither report should be assumed complete.

### Referral traffic and outcomes

- [ ] Review referral sessions and meaningful actions by source in the approved analytics setup.
- [ ] Check whether visitors land on the expected project, guide or service page.
- [ ] Record qualified enquiries only where attribution is available; do not infer revenue from a backlink alone.
- [ ] Investigate sudden referral spikes for bots or irrelevant traffic.

### New and lost links

- [ ] Record new referring domains, source URL, target URL, anchor/context, link date and relationship source.
- [ ] Verify that earned links resolve directly to the canonical HTTPS URL and are not broken.
- [ ] Review lost links: restore only when the relationship/resource remains valid; do not pressure editors.
- [ ] Update redirected or removed Sky Designers URLs where an important referring page still points to them.
- [ ] Watch for duplicate directory profiles, inaccurate contact data and exact-match anchor repetition.

### Branded visibility and entity consistency

- [ ] Review Search Console queries containing `Sky Designers` and common legitimate brand variations.
- [ ] Check branded results manually in a neutral session for outdated profiles, incorrect contact details or impersonation.
- [ ] Verify the six official profiles still link to the canonical site and use current brand details.
- [ ] Record branded impressions/clicks as directional evidence, not proof that a specific backlink caused growth.

### Response rules

- A poor-looking link does not automatically require action. Document it and assess whether there is a manual-action, security, impersonation or material manipulation concern.
- Request correction or removal when an inaccurate profile is under Sky Designers' control or a genuine publisher has made a material error.
- Do not begin routine disavowal activity without clear evidence of manipulative links and an informed review of current search-engine guidance.
- Report monthly on legitimate new domains, valuable referral sources, lost priority links, citation corrections, outreach progress and the next small set of actions.

## Quality gate for every opportunity

Proceed only when all applicable answers are satisfactory:

- Is the site and page relevant to a Sri Lankan business, marketing, design, technology, education, event or client audience?
- Is there a real editorial, membership, client, partner or resource relationship?
- Will the link help a visitor understand the cited company, project or resource?
- Are the business details and claims accurate and approved?
- Is the placement visible and not disguised?
- Can Sky Designers accept a nofollow or platform-selected link attribute without trying to manipulate it?
- Would the opportunity still be worthwhile for reputation, referral traffic or relationship value if search rankings did not change?

If the last answer is no, deprioritise the opportunity.
