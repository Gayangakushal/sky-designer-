## Evidence standard

This audit separates three kinds of information:

1. **Stored project evidence:** client/brand, services, scope, delivery statements, media and descriptions explicitly present in the published record.
2. **Promotional subject copy:** product, event, course or offer wording shown in the creative. It can describe what the asset says, but it is not evidence of Sky Designers' project objective, service scope or outcome.
3. **Outcome evidence:** campaign period, source, attribution method and verified business or platform result. No published `/work` record currently contains this evidence.

A publication date is not treated as a campaign period. A YouTube/Google Drive embed is not treated as proof that YouTube was the campaign platform. Product prices, course hours, product claims, phone numbers and event statements in creative copy are not Sky Designers performance metrics.

They should not be copied onto a `/work` page unless an exact project match and client approval are verified.

## Manual content workflow for stronger future cases

Before upgrading a project to a full case study:

1. Confirm the named client and obtain permission for the public narrative.
2. Record the business context, objective and real challenge in the client's words.
3. List Sky Designers' exact responsibilities and exclusions.
4. Record the delivery/campaign period separately from publication dates.
5. Obtain approved screenshots, creative or before/after material.
6. Define every metric, source, attribution window and comparison period.
7. Separate platform-attributed, analytics-tracked, CRM-confirmed and payment-confirmed outcomes.
8. State limitations and avoid implying the result is typical.
9. Obtain final approval for names, quotes, claims and visuals.
10. Update visible content and structured data together.
