## Cannibalization decisions

- The homepage owns **digital marketing agency sri lanka**. The About page is differentiated around branded company information rather than the generic agency query.
- The Services overview owns **digital marketing services sri lanka**. Individual service pages target their specialist service categories.
- The homepage may mention creative and social media capabilities as supporting context, while the branding and social-media detail pages remain the primary destinations for those specialist searches.
- The Performance Advertising page owns the professional service intent. Packages is differentiated around package selection and pricing, not agency/service discovery.
- Work owns portfolio discovery. Individual work URLs retain project-specific titles and intent.
- The existing `/graphic-design` URL remains a gallery with a branded visual-example intent; the Branding & Creative Design service page owns the commercial branding-agency query.
- Blog owns the insights-hub intent. Individual article URLs retain topic-specific intent through their stored SEO fields and article content.
