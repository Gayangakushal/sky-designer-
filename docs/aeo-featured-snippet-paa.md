# Sky Designers Featured-Snippet and People Also Ask Plan

Audit date: 2026-09-01  
Market: Sri Lanka  
Phase: AEO Step 2 — local optimization only

## 1. Scope and selection rule

This plan selects ten P1 questions for which an existing local draft is already the clearest answer owner. It does not create additional landing pages or expand every possible FAQ. Selection is based on commercial relevance, answer safety, existing content depth, distinct intent, and the ability to give a useful answer without inventing prices, policies, inclusions, results, or market averages.

## 2. Selected P1 snippet targets

### Target 1 — Meta advertising cost

- **Target question:** What affects Facebook and Instagram advertising cost in Sri Lanka?
- **Primary URL:** `/blog/facebook-instagram-ads-cost-sri-lanka`
- **Local source:** `docs/blog-drafts/02-facebook-instagram-ads-cost-sri-lanka.md`
- **Answer format:** Paragraph
- **Recommended answer:** Facebook and Instagram advertising cost depends on the objective, audience, competition, campaign period, creative requirements, testing plan, conversion path and measurement setup. The total should separate media or platform spend from the agency or management fee, creative production and any approved third-party or conversion-path costs. Compare quotations using the same scope and period, not one headline total.
- **Supporting evidence:** Performance Advertising service scope and current Packages context. Published campaign analyses provide campaign-specific measurement context but are not pricing benchmarks.
- **Safety boundary:** Do not add a universal LKR range, standard cost per lead, tax/payment claim, or package value without dated verification.

### Target 2 — branding cost

- **Target question:** What affects branding cost in Sri Lanka?
- **Primary URL:** `/blog/branding-cost-sri-lanka`
- **Local source:** `docs/blog-drafts/05-branding-cost-sri-lanka.md`
- **Answer format:** Paragraph
- **Recommended answer:** Branding cost depends on the problem being solved, research and discovery, creative direction, identity elements, required applications, guideline depth, feedback stages, revisions and final deliverables. A logo-only assignment and a complete identity system therefore should not be compared as the same project. Review the defined scope, ownership and handover files before comparing quotation totals.
- **Supporting evidence:** Branding & Creative Design service scope. Current work shows creative output but not a verified complete branding engagement.
- **Safety boundary:** Confirm actual revisions, deliverables, ownership, and handover policy before publication; do not add price bands.

### Target 3 — video production cost

- **Target question:** What affects video production cost in Sri Lanka?
- **Primary URL:** `/blog/video-production-cost-sri-lanka`
- **Local source:** `docs/blog-drafts/06-video-production-cost-sri-lanka.md`
- **Answer format:** Paragraph
- **Recommended answer:** Video production cost depends on pre-production, concept and scripting, filming time, crew, equipment, locations, talent, editing, sound, motion graphics, licensing, revisions and final deliverables. A repeatable social content session and a campaign or brand production require different resources. Compare quotations using the same production conditions, usage requirements and delivery list rather than video duration alone.
- **Supporting evidence:** Video & Content Production service scope and published production outputs, including the OMS T30 project. Outputs are not pricing or performance evidence.
- **Safety boundary:** Do not state standard crew sizes, filming days, asset quantities, turnaround times, licensing terms, or prices without a defined approved scope.

### Target 4 — website development cost

- **Target question:** What affects website development cost in Sri Lanka?

- **Local source:** `docs/blog-drafts/07-business-website-cost-sri-lanka.md`

### Target 5 — choosing a Meta Ads agency

- **Target question:** How do you choose a Meta Ads agency in Sri Lanka?

- **Local source:** `docs/blog-drafts/01-choose-meta-ads-agency-sri-lanka.md`

- **Safety boundary:** Account ownership, access, reporting cadence, and handover statements require confirmation as current Sky Designers policy before publication.

## 4. Service-page optimization decision

No additional service-page FAQs were added in Step 2.

All six service pages already contain:

- an answer-first introduction;
- a definition, audience, and business problem;
- a direct scope/cost paragraph;
- a three-point provider-evaluation checklist;
- service approach and scope;
- related work with evidence limitations;
- two concise service-specific FAQs.

Adding four to six visible FAQs per service would shift the pages toward informational article intent and duplicate the selected P1 drafts. The PAA map is therefore a routing plan: the commercial page answers service fit, while the local draft owns detailed cost, comparison, planning, or provider-selection questions.

## 6. Published blog and template audit

The seven published bodies remain external to this repository and were not read from or written to the Blog API. The local article template already provides:

- a visible H1, author, publication/update dates, and article body;
- readable `.blog-prose` styling for stored headings, paragraphs, lists, and tables;
- relevant service, work, and article links when configured;
- BlogPosting structured data aligned with visible content.

No template change was made. A template-generated answer or FAQ would risk changing the meaning of seven different articles, duplicating body content, or displaying a claim not written by the article author. Snippet improvements to a published body should be made only through a reviewed editorial update to that individual article.

## 9. Safety rules

- Do not invent Sri Lankan price ranges, averages, cost-per-result benchmarks, timelines, or allocations.
- Meta cost answers must distinguish media/platform spend, agency or management fees, creative/production, and approved third-party costs where applicable.
- Website cost answers must distinguish design/development, content/SEO, domain/hosting, integrations, maintenance, and recurring costs where applicable.
- Video cost answers must distinguish pre-production, production, post-production, and relevant talent/location/equipment/licensing factors.
- Branding cost answers must distinguish research/scope, identity development, applications, guidelines, revisions, deliverables, and ownership.
- Phrase service inclusions conditionally unless the current public scope verifies them.
- Do not turn a portfolio output into a performance result or price benchmark.
- Do not infer a policy for account, domain, code, data, source-file, or intellectual-property ownership; verify it before publication.
- Do not claim awards, rankings, guarantees, typical results, client approval, or undisclosed platform capabilities.
- Do not add FAQPage schema automatically. Visible FAQs can remain without FAQ schema.
- Preserve Organization, WebSite, AboutPage, Service, CreativeWork, and BlogPosting structured data unless visible content supports a reviewed change.
- Do not create hidden answer blocks, custom AEO schema, or copy labelled for AI/search systems.
- One primary page owns each major question; supporting pages should link contextually rather than reproduce the full answer.
